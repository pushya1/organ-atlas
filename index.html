01 · Meet your heart

A small engine with a huge job

Your heart sits in the middle of your chest, tilted a little to the left and protected by your ribs. Its job is to keep blood moving, so every cell in your body gets the oxygen and food it needs.

02 · Four rooms, two pumps

The four chambers

The heart is really two pumps side by side. The right side sends blood to the lungs; the left side sends it to the whole body. A wall of muscle called the septum keeps them apart. Each side has a small top chamber (an atrium) that receives blood and a big bottom chamber (a ventricle) that pumps it out.

👀 Heart diagrams show the heart as if you’re facing someone, so the heart’s right side appears on the left of the picture.

03 · The journey of blood

Follow one blood cell around your body

Blood travels in a figure of eight: a short loop to the lungs to pick up oxygen, and a long loop around the body to deliver it. Every complete trip passes through the heart twice.

    05 · The spark

    The heart’s own electricity

    Your heart doesn’t wait for your brain to tell it to beat. It has its own built-in electrical wiring that fires about once every second.

    1. 1

      The pacemaker fires

      The SA node, a tiny patch of special cells in the right atrium, sends out an electrical signal.

    2. 2

      Atria squeeze

      The signal spreads across both atria like a ripple, and they contract.

    3. 3

      A tiny pause

      The AV node holds the signal for about a tenth of a second, so the ventricles have time to fill up.

    4. 4

      Ventricles squeeze

      The signal races down the septum and spreads through the ventricle walls. They squeeze from the bottom up, like squeezing toothpaste out of a tube.

    07 · Look after it

    Keep your heart happy

    🏃

    Move every day

    Aim for about an hour of activity that makes you a bit out of breath. Your heart is a muscle, and exercise makes it stronger.

    🥦

    Eat colourful food

    Fruit, vegetables, whole grains and nuts help keep your blood vessels clear. Go easy on salty snacks and sugary drinks.

    😴

    Sleep well

    Your heart rate slows while you sleep, giving your heart a rest. Most young people need 8–10 hours.

    🚭

    Say no to smoking and vaping

    Smoke and nicotine damage blood vessels and make the heart work much harder.

    💧

    Drink water

    Blood is about half water. Staying hydrated makes it easier for your heart to pump.

    😄

    Laugh and relax

    Stress makes your heart race. Time with friends, hobbies and a good laugh are good for it.
